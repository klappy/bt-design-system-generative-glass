Overview

One glass language for every Bible Translation app. Scripture stays legible; everything around it is quiet.

Generative Glass is the shared system behind 3D Review, the FIA companion, Aquifer Window and the apps that follow. Surfaces are translucent frosted glass over a slow aurora, edges are hairlines of light, depth is diffuse. The aurora and the glass itself are parametric: they respond to the passage, its script, and where a project is in its process. The apps have different building blocks; the material, the type, the motion and the voice are the same.

Color

Four families. Ink for text, paper for the studio, aurora for what lives behind glass, and an accent budget of exactly three colors. Status is carried by aurora tint and by shape, never by a fifth accent. Saturation arrives only through imagery.

Glass

Every surface carries three things at once: a 0.5px white hairline, an inset top light, and a faint prismatic sheen on the diagonal. Blur only where a surface floats over content it does not own. Never more than two glass levels stacked. Scripture always sits on fill 3 or higher.

Motion

Organic and viscous. Nothing snaps, nothing fades linearly. Sequenced in three beats: what leaves softens, what connects draws, what arrives rises. A particle ring precedes anything about to expand.

Icons

Library · Bible Translation

LanguagePicker

One picker for every place a language is chosen: the app's own locale, a filter on resources, an attribute on a project or passage, the target of a translation, or a gateway pair. Thousands of languages, so the list is never browsed cold: a Suggested group comes first (recent, then this person's project languages, then the device locale, then gateway languages), and typing matches autonym, English name, code, region and alternate names. Every row carries coverage per resource type in three states, and the row itself never lies about what exists.

Universe by context
Filter hides languages with nothing in the current source. Attribute, target, pair show every language; empty ones read “no resources yet” and stay selectable. UI locale shows every language; ones the app is not localized in fall back to a gateway language with a note.
Coverage, per resource type
Chips for Bible · Notes · Words · Maps light up per language. Available is a filled chip. AI-translatable is the chip with an AI tag: a resource an LLM handles well that can be produced on demand. None is a dashed hairline. AI is one rung on a confidence ladder (draft, checked, published) that resource metadata will carry later; the mark is designed to grow into that ladder.
Row and data
Autonym first in its own script and direction, English name, then code in mono. List is the union of Door43 /languages and Aquifer localizations, keyed by BCP-47. Sorted by English name after the Suggested group, since autonyms cannot share one alphabet. Sheet on mobile, popover on desktop, same rows.

Generative rules

The aurora is seeded by scripture: testament sets the hue family, genre sets the blob layout. The glass is parametric: passage length sets fill, script sets blur, process stage tints the sheen, nesting depth steps the radius. Every output snaps to a ladder step, so the result is always a token, never an arbitrary value.

Rule 1 · Aurora from scripture

Old Testament: sky and lavender, cool. New Testament: peach and lemon, warm. Genre places the blobs: Law is a steady horizon, Poetry pools low, Prophecy rises, Gospel centers, Epistle spreads evenly, Apocalyptic is high and asymmetric.

Rule 2 · Fill from length

Scripture never sits below fill 3. Up to 8 verses: fill 3. Longer: fill 4. UI cards keep the full ladder from fill 1.

Rule 3 · Blur from script

Scripts with diacritics, points or conjuncts need a calmer ground. Latin: medium. Greek: medium. Hebrew with points, Arabic: strong. Devanagari: strong. Any script at caption size: heavy.

Rule 4 · Sheen from stage, radius from depth

Understand tints the refraction sky, Translate peach, Revise and check lavender. Stage names are per process. Each nesting level steps the radius 28 → 22 → 18 → 14; two glass levels max.

Generative · concept

Project constellation

A partner's projects as a drifting field instead of a list. Each project is a glass orb whose inner aurora is generated from its state. Clusters sit where their region is: orbs are placed on a Natural Earth projection, and a hairline world outline can be drawn under them or switched off. Click a region name to zoom to it, World to return. For a coordinator with forty projects this is the home screen; for a translator with one it is a single orb.

Content

Voice and copy

Calm, second person, short. The app speaks to one named person and never about itself. It never grades a team; it shows bands and asks questions. Scripture is quoted exactly and never paraphrased in UI copy.

Rules
CasingSentence case. Overlines uppercase at 9px. Book names and references as the translation writes them: Ruth 2:3, 1 Cor 13.
LengthTitles two lines max. Supporting copy one sentence. Survey questions one sentence, one idea.
ResultsBands, never raw scores. Simple and detailed levels for different readers. Findings are proposed until the team ratifies them.
CadenceNever prescribe one. "Repeat when it is appropriate." Point to stage guidance, not a universal schedule.
RolesTranslator, Community, Church, Consultant, Viewer. Participants are never called users.
NeverEmoji, exclamation marks, em dashes, "I", grades, percentages on people, "AI" in participant-facing copy.
