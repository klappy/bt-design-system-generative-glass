Pill-shaped action. Always fully rounded — this system has no square buttons.

```jsx
<GlassButton variant="solid" full onClick={open}>Open</GlassButton>
<GlassButton variant="night" leading={<Icon name="navigation" size={18}/>}>Route</GlassButton>
```

Hover lifts 1px and brightens 4%; press scales to .972. `solid` is reserved for the primary action sitting on a photo; `dark` for the single send/confirm affordance on light glass.
