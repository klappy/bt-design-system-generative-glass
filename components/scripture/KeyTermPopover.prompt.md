A floating glass card that defines one key term, cites its Aquifer or Door43 source, and offers "Open in glossary".

```jsx
<KeyTermPopover source="Aquifer Open Bible Dictionary · 8076" label="Ruth" definition="A Moabitess and the widow of Mahlon…" onClose={close}/>
```

Position it absolutely above the chip that opened it. Definitions are quoted from the resource, never rewritten.
